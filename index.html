Ask a question

What services does Ardur Technology provide?

Five core lines: US mortgage appraisal support, records management, airline record management, knowledge process outsourcing (KPO) and non-voice process outsourcing. Each can run on its own or as part of a bundled engagement.

How do we get started?

Every engagement starts with a short discovery call. We map your volumes and turn times, then share a clear scope and quote before any work begins.

Can you work with our existing systems and team?

Yes. We work inside your LOS, DMS, maintenance tracking or ticketing tool, follow your SOPs and report to your managers, so it feels like an extension of your own team.

How long does a typical project take?

Discovery takes about a week. Most pilots go live within two weeks, and full volume typically follows within six to eight weeks.

Do your teams perform licensed appraisals?

No. Licensed appraisers sign the reports. We handle the review, QC, data validation, order management and coordination work around them, which is where most turn time is lost.

How is pricing structured?

Per file, per record or per dedicated full-time resource, whichever matches your volume pattern. You get a clear quote after discovery, before any work begins.
