Mortgage Appraisal

Cutting appraisal turn times for a US wholesale lender

Client

US wholesale lender (confidential)

Industry

Mortgage lending

Marketing ROI:

31% faster turn times

Timeline:

12 weeks to full volume

The lender was losing lock extensions to appraisal queues. Review backlogs built up overnight, revisions looped for days, and the in-house team had no visibility into where files actually sat.

On the surface the process worked: files moved, reports came back. Underneath, every exception was handled manually, and every manual touch added a day.

Discovery

We started with a two-week discovery on live files: mapping volumes by channel, measuring where hours were lost between order, review, revision and delivery.

The pattern was clear. Roughly 70% of review touches were routine data validation and comp checks, work that never needed a licensed reviewer but always landed on their desk.

Intervention

We took over the review queue with a trained appraisal support team working the lender's own checklists inside their LOS. Files arrived pre-validated, exceptions were bundled into one morning escalation, and revisions came back with the fix documented.

  • Overnight validation of every incoming appraisal file
  • Exception bundling with one morning escalation window
  • Revision turnaround measured in hours, not days
  • Weekly SLA reporting straight into the lender's dashboard

“The queue stopped being the reason files hit lock extension. We see exceptions once a day, decided in one sitting.”

VP of Operations
US wholesale lender/div>

Impact

Beyond the immediate turn-time gains, the lender gained a queue that scales with volume. Pilot files became a standing team in twelve weeks, and the exception process now absorbs seasonal spikes without new hiring.
